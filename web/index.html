    Review before signing

    You are signing an institutional attestation on the chain. Review every line — this statement becomes part of the kit's permanent custody record.

    1. Save the unsigned transaction below and sign it with the institution key: cp tx.json signed.json, then gnokey sign -tx-path signed.json -chainid <network> -account-number N -account-sequence M <key-name>
    2. Broadcast it — either yourself (gnokey broadcast -remote <rpc> signed.json) and paste the transaction hash below, or paste the whole signed document and let the configured relay transmit it.
    3. Press Check chain receipt — a case is confirmed only when the realm's own receipt for this exact operation is visible.
